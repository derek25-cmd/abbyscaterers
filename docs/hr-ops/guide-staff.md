# Staff guide — using the system

This page is for everyone who signs in with a **Staff** login.

## What you can do

- **Apply for your own leave** and follow your requests.
- **See your leave balance**: how many days you have left this year.
- **See your attendance**: the days and times recorded for you.
- **Record training** where your work needs it.

## Once you submit, it is final

After you submit something, you cannot change it or delete it. If something is wrong, ask HR to correct it.

## Attendance is recorded at the attendance kiosk

You clock in when you arrive and clock out when you leave, at the attendance kiosk (the tablet at your site). You no longer enter or correct attendance yourself in this system. On the Attendance page you can see your own days. If a day is wrong, for example a missing clock-out, ask HR to correct it.

## How to apply for leave

1. Sign in and open **Leave**.
2. Click **Apply for leave**.
3. Choose the **type** of leave (for example Annual or Sick).
4. Choose the **first** and **last** day. The system counts the working days for you; Sundays and public holidays are not counted.
5. Add a short reason if you want to.
6. For **Sick**, **Maternity** or **Paternity** leave, attach the document (a medical note, for example). You cannot submit without it.
7. Click **Send request**.

Your request now shows as **Pending**. HR or a manager will approve or reject it. You get a notification (the bell at the top) when they decide.

- **Approved:** the days come off your balance.
- **Rejected:** the notification tells you why.
- **Changed your mind?** You can **Cancel** a request while it is still pending. Once it is approved, ask HR to cancel it.

## Things to know

- You can only apply for your own leave.
- You cannot ask for more days than you have left.
- A request cannot overlap leave you already have, or run across 31 December. Make two requests instead.

Questions? Ask the HR Manager.
